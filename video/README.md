# The Magical Rainbow Bus — Animal Friends Adventure

A 3-minute (3:06) 16:9 preschool animation segment for Learnzo, ages 2–6.
Everything here is generated by the scripts in this folder, so the episode can be
re-rendered or re-timed without touching the art.

## Deliverables

| File | What it is |
| --- | --- |
| `The-Magical-Rainbow-Bus.mp4` | Final 1920×1080 / 30 fps video (3:06, 43 MB) with the soundtrack: voice, original music, SFX and captions. |
| `magical-rainbow-bus-poster.png` | Key-art poster still. |
| `assets/learnzo-logo.png` | The supplied Learnzo logo, used unmodified (see below). |

## Where the supplied logo is used

The attached `Learnzo_transparent.png` is copied byte-for-byte to
`assets/learnzo-logo.png` and is the **exact** artwork composited into the film —
no logo is redrawn or regenerated.

1. **Bus-stop sign**, Scene 1 — the logo is perspective-mapped onto the blank board
   of the village bus shelter (both the wide village plate and the close-up plate),
   with a multiply-style blend so the board's own lighting reads through it.
2. **Maggie's side decal** — a small, slightly tilted copy on the side panel, just
   behind the rainbow stripes.
3. **Title card** and **end card** lock-up.

## Characters

All six share one art source (`assets/ref_characters_magenta.png`, generated once)
and are keyed out once into `assets/char_*.png`, so proportions, colours and facial
detail never drift between shots:

* **Maggie** — golden-yellow bus, big blue eyes in the windshield, smiling chrome bumper, rainbow side stripes, Learnzo decal.
* **Bunny** — white rabbit, sky-blue bow.
* **Buddy** — light-brown puppy, red collar.
* **Leo** — lion cub, green scarf.
* **Ellie** — grey elephant, purple scarf.
* **Miko** — brown monkey, yellow cap.

## Voice track status

Scene 1 is fully voiced (Maggie, Bunny, Buddy, Leo, Ellie, Miko), plus song 1,
Maggie's rainbow reveal and the first quiz question. The remaining scripted lines
— the seven colour calls, song verse 2, the friends verse, the second quiz
question and the closer — are currently carried by the on-screen lyrics/captions
and the music bed, because the text-to-speech quota for this session was reached
mid-build. `plan.py` already lists all of them in `OPTIONAL`; dropping the audio
files into `audio/` and re-running `make_audio.py` + the mux step inserts them
automatically, with the music auto-ducking around them — no picture re-render
needed.

## Sound

`make_audio.py` synthesises an original 100 BPM music bed (I–vi–IV–V, bells,
pads, plucks, shaker, claps, kicks, harp glissandi), the sound design (engine,
horn, whooshes, sparkles, chimes, pops, cheer) and ducks the music under every
line of dialogue. No third-party music or samples are used.

## Rebuilding

```bash
pip install numpy pillow opencv-python-headless imageio-ffmpeg   # or use ./pylibs
python3 prep_assets.py     # key the characters, dress the signs, build the decal
python3 make_audio.py      # soundtrack -> build/mix.wav
python3 make_video.py      # picture  -> build/picture.mp4
ffmpeg -i build/picture.mp4 -i build/mix.wav -c:v copy -c:a aac -b:a 192k \
       -shortest The-Magical-Rainbow-Bus.mp4
```

`plan.py` holds the whole timeline (shots, colour beats, voice cues, SFX) and is
shared by both the audio and picture builds, so editing one number keeps sound
and picture in sync.
